# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Thai SME shop and restaurant owners (ร้านอาหาร, ร้านค้าปลีก, ร้านกาแฟ/เบเกอรี่, โรงแรมขนาดเล็ก, และธุรกิจในกลุ่มเดียวกัน) shopping for a POS or self-service system. They arrive comparing options — often mid-research, sometimes referred by word of mouth — and are deciding whether this vendor is trustworthy enough to talk to. Secondary audience: larger B2B/enterprise buyers sourcing at scale, who need more technical/spec detail before contacting sales.

## Product Purpose

Market and sell MYPOS's self-manufactured POS and self-service hardware/software (self-order kiosks, weigh & pay, point of sale, ticketing) to Thai businesses, and convert visitors into consultation/quote requests (contact form, LINE chat). Success is measured by qualified contact/quote submissions, not by online checkout — this is a lead-gen and trust-building site, not e-commerce.

## Brand Personality

Reliable and warm. MYPOS is a Thai manufacturer that owns its whole stack (hardware, software, after-sales support) — the brand should read as an established company with real backing, not a scrappy reseller, while staying approachable and human rather than cold and corporate. Voice: confident and plain-spoken, not salesy or hype-driven.

## Anti-references

Boring corporate/factory websites — generic navy-and-gray B2B sites with stock photography, dense text walls, and no personality. MYPOS should feel more alive and current than that without tipping into flashy e-commerce/marketplace energy (not a Shopee/Lazada-style discount storefront).

## Design Principles

- **Trust through specificity, not decoration.** Prefer real customer photos, real logos, and real numbers over generic stats, gradient badges, or motifs that don't say anything true (a floating icon with no label is worse than no icon).
- **Navigation groups by what the visitor is trying to do**, not by internal site structure — e.g. "by use case" and "by product category" as two honest entry points into the same catalog, low-traffic pages folded into a shared "Resources" menu rather than each claiming top-level space.
- **The brand orange is used with intent**, not applied as a generic AI-default accent — it should feel like it belongs to this manufacturer specifically.
- **Every visual flourish must earn its place.** Motion, badges, and imagery should reinforce a real claim (an actual installation, an actual result) — cut anything decorative-only, however polished it looks in isolation.
- **Thai-first, multilingual-safe.** All three locales (th/en/zh) must render correctly with the same visual quality — typography choices (e.g. avoiding monospace for Thai body/label text) are load-bearing, not cosmetic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

General WCAG AA baseline (contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text, visible focus states). No additional accommodations beyond the existing th/en/zh localization; audience skews general working-age adults, not a specialized access-needs group.
